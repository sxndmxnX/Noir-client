# Enhancements

Alt+A opens settings - allows you to open NOIR's settings in game. reccomend doing this in the base however, as it can cause you to stop moving while the panel is open.

Alt D opens ECP preview - idk why i added this, but its cool i guess. i was bored to be fair

Alt J opens WebSocket Sniffer - i added a WebSocket sniffer that logs all websocket data going in and out. idk why. you can send stuff out too, like a "say" command if you want, but idk. 

CTRL Q opens STARBLAST settings - very useful imo.

Bypass Team Lock - join any teams even if they show locked

Radar Zoom - Increased radar visibility

Fast Respawn - Instant respawn on death

Lowercase Name - pretty obvious no?

Blank Name - simple as shite eh?

Unlock Weapons Store - allows you to contribute in certain modes like MCST and Battle Royale. does not allow for contribution in team modes, you have to be in the base to do that, big ol 🖕 to the starblast backend server.

FOV Zoom - allows mouse wheel to control the FOV. 

Custom Music player - allows for you to play custom tracks, either the built-in ones in the script, or ones you import from yer gitlab or github as .mp3 files.

Strafe (ALT+I) - Enable/disable strafing, it doesn't let you change your angle, it just makes you look like your doing the wiggle wiggle (shakira?)

Survival Timer - Countdown display before sudden death occurs.

True high hue - the best high hue as of now, turns the ASTEROIDS a diff color too :D

Custom Crosshair - can replace the crosshair with a red "+".

Square Sun - Turns the sun into a square shape. cause i can.

Custom Sun Color - can adjust the sun to be whatever color you want.

Sun Size Slider - Adjust sun size as wanted (200-1000)

Remove Stars - Clean black background without stars, also helps with FPS for those with potato PCs.

Crystal Colors - allows you to customize the crystal colors

5-Emotes - pretty obvious yes?

Blank Emotes - adds blank emotes to most modes, team mode, survival, etc. not all modding modes though.

Emote Cycler - Auto-send pre-made sequences of emotes.

Custom Kill Emote - Auto-sends custom message when you kill someone. theres a panel where you can customise it, its red and says "Ke" 

Self-ship-tag - just adds a tag under yer ship lmao.

Kill Counter - logs your kills in a nice panel under the RCS toggle.

Death Counter - logs your deaths in a panel with kill counter. 

Multibox - Spawn up to 5 bots, however if there is other ppl playing on your IP it will decrease to 4-3-2-1 etc. The bots can copy your exact movements. You can kick certain bots or all of them at once. You can solo control specific bots. you can also choose the team for the bots to join (1/2/3) or auto-detect, which is not the best, i recommend manual.

Aimbot - i mean the name is pretty telling no? its lumped together with tracers so they're a pair. press capslock to toggle aimbot, and "]" to toggle tracers on and off. 

Missile Spam / Unlimited Weapons - Currently patched, will fix soon

Unlock Upgrades - Currently patched, might now be server authenticated. might not work anymore sadly.

Emote Cap Slider — Allows you to set the max number of emotes you can send, from 1 to 5 in realtime. 

Smooth Gems - Them gems be SMOOVE now.

Diamond Sun - is obvious no?

Custom ECP Skins - adds a boatload of other cool ecp skins. goes well with custom badges

NOIR User List - Shows all other NOIR users currently active.

Serverlist - A built in serverlist that lists all avaliable sectors. (mostly works, but some parts are still in progress and may be buggy as of now, dont flame me if you find errors.)

Music player - replaces the built in music with about 300 other tracks that you can go through, toggle is ALT M.

Custom Badges (SRC Champ, SDC Champ, Developer, plus a crapton of other badge images) only you and other ppl using this client can see them btw.

See blank badges - allows ECPs set on blank to be visible, like everyones, not just yours. 

Ship path finder - lets you see all the ships in the game, allows you to set targets and tells you how to get it. plus works in MCST too btw, made it specifically for that mode.

Freecam - allows you to detach the camera from your ship and drag around using right mouse btn. Alt V toggles on and off. you can also zoom in n out.

Rumble Healing - rumble used to have a heal function, still in there, so i hooked it, after you die and respawn, you have 6 seconds to press "-" (minus key) and it will toggle healing on and off. yeah i love coding.

Custom Laser skins - modifies the existing laser skins to make them look cooler. yeah i'm fully out of ideas now bro, even i can tell.

Coordinate tracker - allows you to view your world coordinates. useful i think

No more anon ships - Removes anonymous ships in PDM, basically when you cannot see the names in PDM, now you can.

Gamepad Support - adds gamepad support to the browser, works the exact same, mostly anyways. yeah.

Anti-Kick - sets the timer that automatically kicks you to a 999, it wont last forever, but it will last longer than one minute.

FPS Unlock - Removes frame rate cap, only works on high-end PCs that can support higher FPS than you currently run.

Disable Explosions - Improves performance ig. 

Killfeed - Shows a live feed of who killed who in the top right corner, lowk goated i am MMMMMMMMMM.

Galaxy Map - adds a galaxy map where you can view all sectors, just like in the standalone. BTW, when you`re in the galaxy map n you wanna leave it, press alt g to leave. thanks @Zyrvaen for writing this, i just modded it to work in NOIR. theres also credit to him in NOIR's about section.

ECP Creator - Press the ECP icon in next to NOIR's settings to open a full ECP Maker. customize your badge, hull material, and laser type. got like 100+ materials, animated skins, custom image uploads, and live in-game previews (only you see it tho, you know the drill, FUCK STARBLAST'S SERVER). Also ported this from @Zyrvaen, thanks my guy.

Ai teammate bot - a bot that will follow you around, and do what you command it to do. if you attack someone, it'll attack them too. it is still a work in progress, working on the auto mine and attacking functions for maximum efficiency, but they are almost fully done, just need to add some final exceptions and logic to it :) 

Custom weapon and station models - customises how the stations and weapons look.

always new features otw....
